Privacy Policy

Application: WHOOP Personal WeChat
Effective date: 2 November 2026

1. About this application

WHOOP Personal WeChat (the "App") is a private, single-user application for the account holder's personal health and fitness tracking. It is not offered to the public and has no public user registration. The App is not operated for an employer or business, and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by WHOOP, PushPlus, or WeChat.

2. Information accessed

With the account holder's explicit authorization through WHOOP OAuth, the App may access the following categories of data, subject to the permissions actually granted and data made available by WHOOP:

  • Sleep: duration, timing, stages, and sleep performance.
  • Recovery: recovery score, heart rate variability (HRV), and resting heart rate.
  • Activity: daily strain, physiological cycles, and available heart-rate metrics.
  • Workout: exercise type, duration, and workout-related metrics.
  • Authentication: OAuth access and refresh tokens used to maintain the authorized connection.

3. How the information is used

Information is processed only to create personal daily sleep, recovery, and fitness summaries, identify trends, and optionally deliver a daily summary to the account holder's own WeChat account. It is not used for advertising, marketing, profiling other people, or sale to third parties. Reports provide general wellness information and are not medical advice. By default, the App analyzes data locally and does not send WHOOP data to an AI provider. If the account holder expressly enables optional personal OpenAI API analysis, selected current-day wellness metrics and aggregated historical trends are transmitted to OpenAI over HTTPS to generate a personalized written summary. This optional feature may incur separate API charges, can be disabled locally at any time, and is not the same as the account holder’s ChatGPT conversation. WHOOP passwords and OAuth tokens are not transmitted to the AI provider.

4. Local storage and security

The App is designed to operate on the account holder's personal Windows computer. Retrieved WHOOP records, generated summaries, and application logs are stored locally, without an App-operated cloud database. Authentication credentials and tokens are handled by the local application; access to the computer and its user account should be protected. Connections to WHOOP and the notification service use HTTPS. No system can guarantee absolute security.

5. Optional WeChat notifications and third parties

If notifications are enabled, the App sends a selected daily summary through PushPlus to the account holder's personal WeChat account. The message may include sleep duration, recovery score, HRV, resting heart rate, strain, and similar wellness metrics. PushPlus and WeChat can process and retain messages according to their own privacy policies and service terms. The App does not intentionally transmit a complete WHOOP data archive, WHOOP password, or OAuth tokens to those notification services.

The App does not require Dropbox, Google Drive, an employer's accounts, or employer-managed infrastructure.

6. Retention and deletion

Locally stored data remains on the personal device until the account holder deletes it or removes the App and its data folder. The account holder may stop new collection by revoking the App's WHOOP authorization and disabling the scheduled synchronization task. Deleting local files does not automatically remove messages already transmitted to PushPlus or WeChat; retention of those messages is controlled by the respective providers.

7. Consent and control

WHOOP data access requires the account holder's authorization. Authorization can be revoked through WHOOP account or connected-app settings. WeChat notifications can be disabled locally. If the App is later made available to other users or its processing changes materially, this policy will be reviewed and updated before that change.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the App's functionality, service providers, or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when changes are made.
